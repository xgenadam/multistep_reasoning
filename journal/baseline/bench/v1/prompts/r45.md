I am the product manager for the Hearthline T2, a smart thermostat for homes with a gas or oil boiler. Firmware 1.4 is shipping, and we need the full user manual, which will be published online and as a printed booklet. Please write it, about 7,000 words.

The readers are homeowners, not engineers. Write in a friendly, clear voice; use short sentences; explain any technical word the first time it appears; and give exact menu paths in the form **Menu > Heating > Schedule**. Describe only the features and settings below, with the ranges and defaults exactly as given; do not invent features, settings, error codes or specifications.

**Product facts.**
- The T2 has a wall unit with a 3.5-inch touch screen and a rotating ring, a receiver box wired next to the boiler, and the Hearthline app for iOS and Android.
- The wall unit runs on 2 AA batteries (about 2 years of life) or 24 V AC wiring; the receiver box is mains powered.
- Wi-Fi: 2.4 GHz only. The receiver and wall unit talk to each other by radio at up to 30 m indoors.
- Installation of the receiver box must be done by a qualified installer; the manual covers setup after that.

**The twelve features, each with its own chapter, in this order, with these titles as chapter headings:**

1. Getting Started: pairing the wall unit with the receiver; connecting to Wi-Fi; creating an account in the app. Settings: Language (English, French, German, Spanish; default English), Temperature unit (°C or °F; default °C), Time format (12 h or 24 h; default 24 h).
2. Manual Temperature Control: turn the ring or use the app. Settings: Target temperature (5–30 °C in 0.5 °C steps; default 20 °C), Hold duration (until next schedule change, 1–12 hours, or permanent; default until next schedule change).
3. Weekly Schedule: Settings: Periods per day (1–6; default 4), Copy day (to any other days), Default periods (06:30 20 °C, 08:30 17 °C, 17:00 21 °C, 22:30 16 °C).
4. Away Mode and Geofencing: heating drops to the eco temperature when every household member's phone has left the area. Settings: Geofence radius (200–2,000 m; default 500 m), Return pre-heat (on/off; default on).
5. Eco Temperature: Settings: Eco temperature (10–20 °C; default 16 °C).
6. Boost: Settings: Boost duration (30, 60, 90 or 120 minutes; default 60 minutes), Boost temperature (default target + 2 °C).
7. Hot Water Control: for homes with a hot-water cylinder. Settings: Hot water schedule (up to 3 periods per day), Hot water boost (30 or 60 minutes; default 60 minutes).
8. Holiday Mode: Settings: Start and end dates, Holiday temperature (5–15 °C; default 12 °C).
9. Frost Protection: heating turns on whenever the room falls below the frost temperature, even when the heating is off. Settings: Frost temperature (5–12 °C; default 7 °C). Frost protection cannot be turned off.
10. Energy Reports: Settings: Weekly email (on/off; default on), Monthly summary in the app (always on), Boiler running hours shown per day.
11. Child Lock: locks the ring and screen on the wall unit. Settings: Lock (on/off; default off), PIN (4 digits; set when lock is first turned on).
12. Multi-User Access: invite household members in the app. Settings: Users (up to 8 per home), Roles (Owner, Member, Guest; Guests cannot change the schedule).
