Write this quarter's engineering newsletter for the roughly 150 engineers at Tidewater, from these twelve facts:

1. The Kestrel checkout service moved to the new payments API on 2 September; all card traffic now goes through it.
2. Checkout p99 latency fell from 840 ms to 310 ms after the move.
3. There were 3 Sev-2 incidents this quarter, down from 7 last quarter, and no Sev-1s.
4. On-call pages averaged 11 a week, down from 26.
5. Priya Raman joined the data platform team as a staff engineer.
6. Tomás Okafor moved from the mobile team to the reliability team.
7. The mobile app's crash-free session rate reached 99.6%.
8. Median CI pipeline time fell from 18 to 9 minutes after test sharding.
9. The monorepo passed 1.2 million lines, and the teams deleted 140,000 lines of dead code.
10. Hack week (6-10 October) produced 14 demos; two of them will ship next quarter.
11. Next quarter the search cluster migrates to the new index format, starting 3 November.
12. The engineering all-hands moves to the first Thursday of each month.

Structure:
- Open with a "By the numbers" section of exactly four bullet points, each one figure from the facts with a few words of context.
- Then five sections with these headings: Shipped, Reliability, People, Under the hood, Coming up.
- Use every fact exactly once across those five sections, in the section where it fits best, and don't repeat material between sections (the four figures in By the numbers may reappear).
- Don't invent facts, quotes or names beyond the twelve facts.

Tone: upbeat but not hype, readable in five minutes. Aim for about 600-800 words.
