I manage customer support at Quillstone, which makes cloud accounting software for small businesses and their bookkeepers. We are hiring twelve new support agents next quarter, and I need a training curriculum for their first two weeks. Please write it, about 7,500 words.

Each module is taught on a different day, often by a different senior agent, and new hires revisit single modules later when they need them. So every module must stand on its own: where a module depends on shared basics (the priority matrix, the response targets, the tone guidelines, the escalation path), restate them in that module rather than pointing to another one. That overlap is intentional.

**Use exactly these ten modules, in this order, each with a heading of the form "Module N: Title":**
1. Welcome to Quillstone Support
2. Our Product in One Day
3. The Ticketing System and Priorities
4. Writing Great Replies
5. Handling Billing and Subscriptions
6. Bank Feeds and Reconciliation Problems
7. Payroll and Tax Questions
8. Difficult Conversations
9. Escalation and Working with Engineering
10. Data Protection and Account Security

**Every module** has exactly five subsections, as headings, in this order:
- "Objectives": exactly three learning objectives, as a numbered list.
- "Content": the teaching material, with concrete examples from Quillstone support.
- "Exercise": one practical exercise (a role-play, a ticket to answer, or a scenario to triage), with what a good answer looks like.
- "Quiz": exactly five questions, as a numbered list. Multiple-choice options, if any, go on lines starting with a letter, such as "A)".
- "Answer Key": the five answers, as a numbered list, each with a one-sentence explanation.

**Shared basics to use.**
- Priority matrix: P1 (a customer cannot use Quillstone at all, or a payroll run is blocked on payday); P2 (a key feature is broken with no workaround); P3 (a problem with a workaround, or a how-to question); P4 (feedback and feature requests).
- First-response targets: P1 30 minutes, P2 2 hours, P3 8 business hours, P4 2 business days.
- Tone guidelines, "CLEAR": Confirm the problem in your own words; Lead with the answer; Explain the steps; Avoid jargon; Recap what happens next.
- Escalation path: Tier 1 (new agents) → Tier 2 (senior agents) → the on-call engineer, only for P1 and P2 issues that Tier 2 cannot resolve.

**Product and policy facts.**
- Plans: Starter €12 per month (1 user, no payroll), Team €29 per month (up to 5 users, payroll for up to 10 employees), Practice €79 per month (for bookkeepers, unlimited client companies).
- Refunds: full refund within 30 days of the first payment on any plan; after that, no refunds for partial months.
- Bank feeds come from a third-party connection service; a broken feed usually needs the customer to re-authorise the connection with their bank, which agents cannot do for them.
- Agents never give tax advice: they explain how to use Quillstone's tax features and refer tax questions to the customer's accountant.
- Identity check before any account change: the agent confirms the account email plus two of the following: the last invoice amount, the company registration number, the date the account was opened.
- Agents never ask for a customer's password, and never share data from one customer's account with another person, even within the same company, without the account owner's permission.
