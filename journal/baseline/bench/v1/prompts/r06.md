**Mobile team weekly, Monday 6 October**
Attendees: Maya (product manager, chair), Owen (backend lead), Lucía (design), Raj (QA lead), Femi (support lead)

**Maya:** Let's start. Three things today: the 4.2 release date, scope, and the beta. Raj, where are we on testing?

**Raj:** Not great. The regression suite is green except for appointment reminders. Patients whose phone is set to a different time zone from their clinic get the SMS reminder at the wrong hour. In one test it went out at 3 a.m.

**Owen:** That's ours. We store the reminder time in the clinic's time zone and then the SMS job converts it a second time. It's a small fix, but it touches the scheduler, so I don't want to rush it.

**Maya:** How long?

**Owen:** I can have it fixed and merged by Thursday.

**Raj:** Then I need a full regression run on the fixed build. That's two days in the device lab, so realistically results on Monday the 13th.

**Maya:** Which leaves one day before the 14th. That's not enough margin. I'd rather move the release a week than ship reminders at 3 a.m. Any objections to the 21st?

**Femi:** None from support. Honestly, a wrong-time reminder generates more tickets than a late release. The last reminder bug cost us about 300 tickets in two days.

**Lucía:** Fine by me.

**Maya:** Decided, then: 4.2 moves to Tuesday 21 October. Owen, the time-zone fix is yours for Thursday. Raj, regression results by Monday the 13th.

**Owen:** Yes.

**Raj:** Yes.

**Maya:** Femi, can your team tell the clinics about the new date?

**Femi:** Sure. I'll draft the customer notice and send it round for review by Friday.

**Maya:** Great. Next, scope. Lucía, dark mode?

**Lucía:** About two-thirds done. The booking calendar still has contrast problems in dark mode, and I don't want to ship something that fails our accessibility checks.

**Maya:** What exactly is left?

**Lucía:** The date picker and the colour-coded appointment slots. Fixing them properly means rethinking how we show availability, and that's more than a week's work on its own.

**Owen:** Even with the extra week I wouldn't count on it.
