**Decisions already made.**
- There will be two regions: region A in Port Alder and region B in Brightwater. Each city is served by its own region in normal operation ("home region").
- If one region fails, the other region takes over that city's traffic. The failover target is: dispatch for the failed city resumes within 5 minutes, with no accepted ride lost.
- Ride state (requests, offers, acceptances, trip status) is replicated asynchronously to the other region. Each ride produces on average 10 ride-state events.
- Location updates are not replicated: after a failover, drivers' apps resend their location to the surviving region, and history for the outage window may be incomplete. Location history stays in the region where it was collected (a data-residency requirement from Brightwater's regulator).
- A rider or driver is pinned to the home region of the city where the ride starts.
