I lead the platform team at Brightwell Logistics and have to submit our FY2027 budget to the finance director. These are the line items:

Cloud infrastructure
- Staging cluster: $2,150 per month for 12 months
- Log storage and retention: $18,600 for the year
- Off-site backup storage: $7,200 for the year

Software licences
- CI service: 12 seats at $420 per seat per year
- Observability platform: $14,400 for the year (a fixed three-year contract; FY2027 is year 2 of 3)
- Code review tool: 12 seats at $280 per seat per year

Training
- Conference attendance: 3 engineers at $2,900 each (ticket, travel and hotel)
- Online course subscriptions: 12 at $360 each
- Internal workshop facilitator: $3,500

Hardware
- Laptop replacements: 4 at $2,450 each
- Test devices: $3,280

Background the finance director will want: the staging cluster caught 9 release-blocking defects last year before they reached customers; our customer contracts require 90 days of log retention; the off-site backups are what our disaster-recovery plan relies on; the four laptops being replaced are five years old and can no longer run the full local build; the conference places go to the three engineers who will run the internal workshop afterwards; the team grows from 10 to 12 engineers in FY2027.

Write the budget proposal with these markdown sections: Summary, Budget table, Justification.

- Budget table: a single table with the columns Category, Line item and Amount, with one row per line item, a subtotal row after each category's line items, and a final grand-total row (16 rows below the header).
- Write every amount in whole dollars with thousands separators, for example $25,800. Every line amount, subtotal and the grand total must be arithmetically correct.
- Justification: one subheading per category, explaining why the money is needed, using the background above.

The tone should be businesslike and confident; the finance director is numerate and short of time. Aim for about 500-700 words.
