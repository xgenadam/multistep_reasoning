I am the product manager for Inkwell, a new note-taking app from a 14-person startup. We have research, personas and a long wish list, and now we need the product requirements document (PRD) that engineering and design will plan the first release from. Please write it, about 6,500 words.

The readers are our engineers, designers and the CEO. Be specific and testable: every requirement should be something a tester could check. Make the trade-offs explicit, especially in the MVP cut.

**Product summary.** Inkwell is a fast, private, offline-first note-taking app for web, iOS and Android. Notes are written in a rich-text editor with Markdown shortcuts, organised in notebooks and tags, and synced across devices. Business model: free for up to 3 devices; Inkwell Plus at €4 per month adds unlimited devices, 10 GB of attachments and version history.

**Personas.**
- Maya, a university student who takes lecture notes on a laptop and reviews them on her phone.
- Daniel, a consultant who keeps meeting notes and needs to find things quickly across years of notes.
- Aiko, a researcher who clips web pages and PDFs and annotates them.

**Use exactly these sections, in this order, as the section headings:**
1. Overview
2. Goals and Non-Goals
3. Personas
4. User Stories
5. Non-Functional Requirements
6. Success Metrics
7. MVP Cut
8. Open Questions

**User Stories.** Write exactly these fifteen user stories, each as a subsection with the heading "US-NN: Title". Each has the story in the form "As a …, I want …, so that …", naming one of the three personas, followed by at least three acceptance criteria, each a single bullet of the form "Given …, when …, then …".
- US-01 Create a note
- US-02 Edit with Markdown shortcuts
- US-03 Organise notes in notebooks
- US-04 Tag notes
- US-05 Full-text search
- US-06 Work offline
- US-07 Sync across devices
- US-08 Attach images and PDFs
- US-09 Clip a web page
- US-10 Annotate a PDF
- US-11 Pin and favourite notes
- US-12 Trash and restore (deleted notes kept for 30 days)
- US-13 Export notes (Markdown and PDF)
- US-14 Version history (Plus only, 30 days of versions)
- US-15 Lock a note with a PIN or biometrics

**Non-Functional Requirements.** Include at least these targets, each as a numbered requirement with an id (NFR-1, NFR-2, …):
- Opening a note under 1 MB: p95 under 300 ms on a mid-range phone.
- Search across 10,000 notes: p95 under 500 ms, working offline.
- Offline edits sync within 10 seconds of the device reconnecting.
- Sync service availability of 99.9% per month.
- Notes encrypted at rest on the device and on the server (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher).
- Accessibility to WCAG 2.1 AA on all platforms.
- Supported platforms: the two latest major versions of iOS and Android, and the latest two versions of the main desktop browsers.

**Success Metrics.** A table with exactly five metrics, with the columns Metric, Definition, Target at 6 months after launch.

**MVP Cut.** The MVP must include exactly eight of the fifteen stories, and US-01, US-02, US-05, US-06 and US-07 must be among them; you choose the other three and justify the choice against the personas and the release date. Use two subsections with these headings: "In the MVP" and "After the MVP", each a bulleted list with one story per bullet, written as "US-NN: Title". Then add a third subsection, headed "Risks of the Cut", that explains the main risks of the cut.

**Open Questions.** List what still needs a decision, as bullets.
