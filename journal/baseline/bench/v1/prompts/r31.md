We're Tallow Analytics, and our data-warehouse contract with Quarry Data expires on 30 June. Our CFO, Dana Whitlock, and our head of data, Ravi Menon, lead the renewal call next week. The facts:

- Current contract: $240,000 a year for 50 user seats with standard support (next-business-day response).
- Quarry's renewal proposal: a 12 % increase on the current annual fee, fixed for a three-year term, with the same seats and support.
- Our alternative: a competitor, Basalt, has quoted $255,000 a year on a three-year term. Migrating would cost about $60,000 one-off in contractor time and take four months, with a real risk of delaying a quarterly reporting cycle.
- What we want: an annual price no higher than today's; 70 seats, because we're hiring; 4-hour response for priority incidents; and the option to leave after year 2.
- What we can offer: a three-year commitment; paying annually in advance (Quarry currently invoices us quarterly); a public case study; acting as a reference customer.
- What we know about Quarry: their account manager mentioned they're pushing to sign multi-year contracts this fiscal year, and their quarter ends on 31 May.

Write a negotiation brief for Dana and Ravi, with these markdown sections: Situation, Our BATNA, Interests, Concessions, Opening position.

- Our BATNA: state the three-year cost of the Basalt alternative including migration, and compare it with the three-year cost of accepting Quarry's proposal as it stands.
- Interests: ours and Quarry's, separately.
- Concessions: a table with at least five rows and the columns Concession, Given by (us or Quarry), Estimated value per year in dollars, and When to trade it.
- Opening position: the specific price, seats, support level and exit terms we open with, and our walk-away point.

Be candid about our weak points as well as our leverage. Aim for about 600-800 words.
