**Facts about Tessellate and PermitFlow (use only these; do not invent others).**
- Tessellate Civic Software: founded 2014, 85 employees, PermitFlow used by 41 local authorities.
- Hosted in two data centres in the same country as the city, 300 km apart, with automatic failover. Measured availability over the last 12 months: 99.93%. Contractual availability offered: 99.9% per month, with service credits.
- Single sign-on with SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect. Multi-factor authentication is built in for all users, and is mandatory for administrators.
- Role-based access control with 12 predefined roles; custom roles are on the roadmap for next year (not available today).
- Full audit log of every view, change and decision, kept for 10 years, exportable as CSV.
- Public applicant portal meets WCAG 2.1 level AA, independently audited last year. The internal staff screens meet most AA criteria; an audit found 14 open issues, to be fixed within 6 months of contract signature.
- Online payment of permit fees through the city's existing payment provider, via a standard integration.
- Electronic plan review: reviewers mark up PDF and image plans in the browser. CAD (DWG) files must be converted to PDF first; there is no native DWG viewing.
- Inspectors' mobile app on iOS and Android. It works offline for viewing assigned inspections, but results can only be submitted when the device is back online (no offline submission).
- Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256). Independent penetration test every year; summary report shared under NDA.
- Open REST API covering applications, inspections and payments, documented publicly. No API for configuring workflows.
- Data migration from the city's current system: done by our team from database exports; we have migrated from the city's current product twice before.
- Reporting: 30 standard reports and a drag-and-drop report builder; reports can be scheduled by email.
- Support: 08:00–18:00 on working days by phone and email; a 24/7 on-call line for severity-1 incidents only (complete outage), with a 30-minute response target.
- Hosting is software-as-a-service only. There is no on-premises version.
- Training: train-the-trainer sessions plus online videos.
