I need a memo for our head of engineering comparing three hosting options for our internal analytics web app, with a recommendation she can approve. She is comfortable with technical detail but wants the money and the trade-offs made plain.

Our workload, per month:
- 3 application instances running around the clock (count a month as 730 hours);
- 500 GB of storage;
- 2 TB of outbound data transfer (count 1 TB as 1,000 GB);
- one managed PostgreSQL database.

Our requirements: an uptime SLA of at least 99.9 %, room to grow to at least 6 application instances, and monthly billing with no contract longer than one month.

The options, as quoted by each vendor:

**Nimbus Cloud** (pay as you go)
- Instances: $0.052 per instance-hour (2 vCPU, 4 GB RAM each), up to 10 instances.
- Storage: $0.10 per GB-month.
- Outbound transfer: the first 1 TB each month is free, then $0.08 per GB.
- Managed PostgreSQL: $95 per month, daily backups included.
- SLA 99.9 %. Monthly billing.

**Harbor Hosting** (Team plan)
- $240 per month flat, including up to 4 instances, 400 GB of storage and 3 TB of outbound transfer.
- Extra storage: $0.15 per GB-month. More than 4 instances needs the Business plan at $410 per month.
- Managed PostgreSQL add-on: $60 per month.
- SLA 99.5 %. Monthly billing.

**Kestrel Platform** (managed app platform)
- Instances: $38 per instance per month, autoscaling up to 20 instances.
- Storage: $0.08 per GB-month.
- Outbound transfer: $0.05 per GB, no free allowance.
- Managed PostgreSQL: $120 per month.
- SLA 99.95 %. Monthly billing.

Use these headings: Options compared, Cost for our workload, Recommendation.

- Under Options compared, give a table with one row per option covering price model, SLA, scaling limit and how well it meets each of our requirements.
- Under Cost for our workload, work out each option's total monthly cost for the workload above, to the cent, and show the line items so the arithmetic can be checked.
- Recommend the cheapest option that meets all of our requirements, and say in a sentence or two what we give up by not choosing the others.

Aim for about 500-700 words.
