Write the press release for our product launch, for agricultural trade press and local business media. Here are the facts; use these and no others.

- Company: Verdane Robotics, based in Halden, Washington. Founded in 2019; 85 employees.
- Product: the Harvester H2, an autonomous apple-picking robot for commercial orchards, the successor to the H1.
- It picks up to 1,200 apples an hour, runs for 16 hours on one battery charge, and bruises 40 % fewer apples than the H1.
- Price: $89,000 per unit; orders open today, and deliveries start in time for next year's harvest.
- It was trialled last season on 27 orchards in Washington and Oregon.
- Release date: 4 May 2027.
- Quote from Ines Albrecht, Verdane's chief executive: she wants to say that labour shortages at harvest are the biggest problem growers have told the company about, and that the H2 is built to work alongside picking crews, not to replace them.
- Quote from Marcus Bell, who runs a family orchard near Wenatchee and took part in the trial: he wants to say that the reduction in bruised fruit is what surprised him most, because bruised apples go to juice at a fraction of the price.
- Media contact: press@verdane.example.

Requirements:
- A headline as a markdown heading, then a dateline (city, state and date) at the start of the first paragraph.
- Two quotations, one from each of the named people above, attributed by name and role, saying what they want to say. You can write the exact wording.
- An "About Verdane Robotics" boilerplate paragraph at the end, followed by the media contact.
- Use each of these figures exactly once in the whole release: 2019, 85, 1,200, 16, 40 %, $89,000 and 27. Don't add any other figures.
- About 400-500 words.

The tone should be factual and confident, the way trade journalists like it, with no superlatives the facts don't support.
