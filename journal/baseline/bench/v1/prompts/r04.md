We're hiring our first backend engineer in over a year and our onboarding is folklore. Write an onboarding checklist for new backend engineers that the hiring manager and the new hire can both work from.

What you need to know about us: Corvid Analytics, a 40-person company; the backend team is six engineers working in Python and PostgreSQL. Code lives on GitHub and every change needs one review. Laptops are set up by IT on the first morning. Each new hire gets an onboarding buddy from the team. We deploy to production several times a day through CI. New engineers join the on-call rotation in their first quarter, after shadowing two on-call shifts. HR runs its own onboarding for payroll, benefits and contracts, so don't mention any of those at all.

Requirements:
- Three phases, under exactly these headings: Week 1, Month 1, Quarter 1.
- Exactly five items per phase, 15 in all, as a numbered list running from 1 to 15 across the phases.
- Every item names its owner, using one of these roles: hiring manager, onboarding buddy, IT support, tech lead, new hire.
- Joining the on-call rotation belongs in Quarter 1, after the two shadow shifts.

Each item should be concrete enough to tick off, with a short note on what "done" looks like where that isn't obvious. Add a one- or two-sentence introduction. Aim for about 400-600 words.
