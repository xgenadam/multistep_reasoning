I run customer support at Ledgerly, a small invoicing app for freelancers (two plans: Solo at $12 a month and Studio at $29 a month, billed by card, monthly or yearly). Our agents need reply templates they can paste into tickets and adjust. Write one template for each of these six situations:

1. A customer can't log in and hasn't received the password-reset email.
2. A customer was charged twice for the same month.
3. A customer on a yearly plan wants to cancel and asks for a refund of the unused part.
4. A customer has lost the phone they use for two-factor authentication.
5. Exporting invoices to PDF fails with an error. This is a known bug with no fix date yet; the workaround is to export to CSV and print from the browser.
6. A customer wants to change the email address that owns the account.

Facts the templates must get right:
- Identity check: before any account change (situations 4 and 6) and before any refund, the customer must confirm the last four digits of the card on file and the date of their most recent invoice. Agents never ask for a full card number or a password.
- Refunds go back to the original card and take 5-7 business days to appear.
- A yearly plan cancelled mid-term is refunded pro rata for whole unused months.
- Password-reset emails come from no-reply@ledgerly.example and are often caught by spam filters; a reset link expires after 30 minutes.
- Two-factor recovery: after the identity check we disable two-factor authentication within one business day, and the customer sets it up again at their next login.

Agents paste exactly one template per ticket, so each template must stand on its own: where situations share steps, such as the identity check or the refund timeline, write the steps out in every template that needs them rather than referring to another template.

Format: give each template a markdown heading naming the situation, then a line starting "Subject:", then the body. Use placeholders in double curly braces. Every template must include {{customer_name}}, {{ticket_id}} and {{agent_name}} (in the sign-off); add others where useful, such as {{refund_amount}}.

Tone: warm, plain and brief, with no blame and no jargon. Don't promise anything the facts above don't support. Aim for about 800-1,000 words in total.
