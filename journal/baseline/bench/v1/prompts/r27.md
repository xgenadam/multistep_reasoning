HR at Fernway, a 300-person logistics software company, wants a short employee pulse survey to run every six months, taking about ten minutes to complete. The four themes are Workload, Manager support, Growth, and Tools and processes. Last year's free-text comments raised three recurring issues: out-of-hours on-call, unclear promotion criteria, and slow laptop replacements.

How responses are handled: the survey runs in an external survey tool; no names or email addresses are stored; results are reported only for groups of seven or more respondents; free-text answers are read only by the HR team and shared only as summarised themes, never quoted.

Write the survey design document for the leadership team to approve. Use these markdown sections: a short Introduction, then one section per theme using the theme names as headings, then a final section, Anonymity and how results are used.

- Exactly twelve questions, three per theme, numbered 1 to 12 continuously across the themes.
- Mix the question types. After each question put its type in square brackets: [Likert], [Multiple choice] or [Open]. Each theme has exactly one [Open] question.
- Likert questions use a five-point scale from "Strongly disagree" to "Strongly agree"; describe the scale once in the Introduction rather than under each question. For multiple-choice questions, give the options in brackets after the question, not as a list.
- Under each question, add a line starting "Why:" saying what decision the answer will inform.
- Questions must be neutral (no leading wording) and each must ask about one thing only.

Aim for about 800-1,000 words.
