**Halvorsen & Pike: Remote and Hybrid Work Policy** (version 3, effective 1 February)

**1. Scope**
1.1 This policy applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees who have completed their probation period of three months. Contractors are covered by the terms of their own contracts.
1.2 Roles marked "on-site" in the role catalogue, including reception, facilities and laboratory technicians, are excluded from sections 2 and 3.

**2. Working pattern**
2.1 Hybrid working is the default. Employees work at least two days a week in the office. Tuesday is the anchor day on which every team is in the office together.
2.2 A fully remote arrangement requires the approval of both the employee's manager and the head of department. Fully remote arrangements are reviewed every 12 months.
2.3 Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00, office local time, during which employees are expected to be available. Outside core hours, employees may arrange their working time flexibly, in agreement with their team.

**3. Location**
3.1 Remote work must be carried out from the employee's country of employment. Working from another country is permitted for up to 20 working days per calendar year, with the approval of HR requested at least 10 working days in advance.
3.2 Employees must give HR the address they usually work from remotely, and update it within 5 working days of any change.

**4. Equipment and costs**
4.1 The company provides each employee with a laptop, a monitor and a headset.
4.2 Employees starting a hybrid or fully remote arrangement may claim a one-off home-office allowance of up to €400 for a desk and chair. The claim must be made within three months of the arrangement starting, with receipts.
4.3 Employees who work remotely at least two days a week receive an internet allowance of €30 per month, paid with their salary.
4.4 Company equipment remains company property and must be returned within 10 working days of the employee's last day.

**5. Information security**
5.1 Company data may be accessed only on company-provided devices. Personal devices must not be used for company data, including email.
5.2 Public or shared Wi-Fi networks may be used only with the company VPN switched on.
5.3 Documents classified as confidential must not be printed outside the office.

**6. Health and safety**
6.1 Employees complete the home-workstation self-assessment within their first month of remote working. The facilities team reviews each assessment and may recommend changes.
6.2 Work-related injuries at home are reported through the same incident process as injuries in the office.

**7. Changes and ending an arrangement**
7.1 Either the employee or the company may end a fully remote arrangement with four weeks' written notice.
7.2 The company may require temporary attendance at the office for business reasons with at least 5 working days' notice, except in emergencies.
7.3 Disagreements about this policy are raised first with HR and, if unresolved, through the grievance procedure.
