Write a job advert for a senior data engineer at Heronfield Health Analytics. We're a 120-person company that turns hospitals' operational data (bed occupancy, theatre schedules, discharge times) into dashboards that 40 hospitals use to cut waiting times. The data platform team has six data engineers and reports to the Head of Data Platform, Amara Osei.

The hiring manager's notes, unsorted:
- strong Python and SQL
- has built and run batch pipelines in production
- Airflow or a similar orchestrator
- dbt would be a plus
- dimensional data modelling
- a cloud data warehouse (we use Snowflake)
- streaming (Kafka) would be nice
- healthcare data experience is nice, not required
- mentors junior engineers
- owns data quality and pipeline testing
- Terraform or other infrastructure-as-code is nice
- explains trade-offs to analysts and clinical staff
- HL7 or FHIR familiarity is nice

Terms: salary £72,000-£88,000 plus a 10 % annual bonus; 30 days' holiday plus bank holidays; hybrid, with two days a week in our Manchester office; a £1,500 yearly learning budget. Interview process: a 30-minute call with Amara, a take-home exercise of no more than three hours, then a panel interview.

Use these markdown section headings: About Heronfield, The role, What you'll do, Must-haves, Nice-to-haves, Salary and benefits, How to apply.

- What you'll do: six to eight bullet points.
- Must-haves: exactly six bullet points; nice-to-haves: exactly four bullet points. Build both from the notes, merging or dropping items as you judge best.
- State the salary band exactly as given.
- Use inclusive language: no "rockstar", "ninja" or "guru", nothing about age or "culture fit", no gendered pronouns for the candidate, and don't ask for a number of years of experience. Say that we encourage people who meet most but not all of the must-haves to apply.

The tone should be warm and specific rather than corporate. Aim for about 500-700 words.
