I am an organisational psychologist at the Institute for Work and Wellbeing, and I am applying to a research foundation for funding for a field experiment on remote and hybrid work. A large insurance company, Calloway Mutual (about 6,000 office staff), has agreed to take part. Please write the full research proposal, about 7,000 words.

The reviewers are academics in psychology, economics and management, plus a lay member of the foundation's board. Write clearly and rigorously; justify each design choice and acknowledge the alternatives. Do not cite specific published studies, authors or statistics from the literature: the foundation's template asks for the background to be described in general terms, and our reference list will be attached separately.

**The study (decisions already made).**
- Two arms: "Hybrid" (three fixed office days a week) and "Remote-first" (office attendance optional, at most one day a week). The comparison is Remote-first against Hybrid.
- Randomisation is by team, not by individual, because work arrangements are set per team. Teams have 8 members on average.
- The intervention runs for 9 months, after a 2-month baseline period.
- Primary outcome: individual productivity, measured as the number of completed work items per week recorded in the company's task-tracking system, standardised within job family.
- Secondary outcomes: wellbeing (the WHO-5 Wellbeing Index, monthly), collaboration (number of distinct colleagues each person exchanged work messages with each month, from metadata only, never content), and voluntary staff turnover.
- Participation is voluntary for individuals; a team takes part if its manager agrees and at least 6 of its members consent.

**Hypotheses.** State exactly three hypotheses, labelled H1, H2 and H3: one for the primary outcome and two for secondary outcomes. State each with its direction.

**Sample Size and Power.** Use these parameters: a standardised effect size (Cohen's d) of 0.3 on the primary outcome; a two-sided significance level of 0.05; power of 0.80; z values of 1.96 and 0.84; an intra-class correlation of 0.05 within teams; an average team size of 8; and 20% attrition over the study. Use the formula n = 2 × (z₁ + z₂)² / d² per group for individual randomisation, then the design effect 1 + (m − 1) × ICC. Do the calculation in these steps, rounding up to a whole number at the end of each step that gives a number of people or teams:
1. per-group sample size for individual randomisation;
2. the design effect;
3. per-group sample size after the design effect;
4. per-group sample size after allowing for attrition (divide by 0.8);
5. teams needed per group (step 4 divided by the team size);
6. total employees enrolled (teams per group × 2 arms × 8).

Put only the number in the Value column.

**Analysis Plan.** Specify a mixed-effects model with a random intercept for team, the primary analysis as intention-to-treat, and how missing data and multiple secondary outcomes will be handled.

**Ethics and Data Protection.** Include consent, the right to withdraw, how productivity and message metadata are pseudonymised, who can see team-level results, and that no individual's results are ever shared with the employer.

**Timeline.** The project lasts 18 months. Give a table with exactly six phases and their months (Month 1 to Month 18), covering set-up, baseline, intervention, analysis and reporting.
