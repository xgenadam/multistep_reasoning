**Programme data for the year.**
- Reading Buddies (trained volunteers read one-to-one with children aged 6–11, weekly, in school): 1,240 children in 18 partner schools, supported by 310 volunteers. Of the 1,240 children, 868 improved their reading age by six months or more over the school year.
- Adult Literacy Classes (small-group evening classes for adults, 20 weeks): 415 adults enrolled and 332 completed the course.
- The Book Bank (free new and donated books at community sites): 26,500 books distributed through 42 community sites, including food banks, clinics and libraries.
- Family Story Nights (evening events at libraries and community centres where parents and children read together): 96 events with 3,840 attendees in total.
- Volunteers across all programmes gave 21,700 hours in total. For reporting, we value volunteer time at €15 per hour.

**Financial data for the year (in euros).**
- Income: grants 612,000; individual donations 284,000; corporate partners 156,000; events and fundraising activities 48,000.
- Expenditure: Reading Buddies 402,000; Adult Literacy Classes 286,000; The Book Bank 118,000; Family Story Nights 64,000; fundraising costs 88,000; governance and administration 77,000.
- Total income in the previous year was 980,000.
- Reserves at the start of the year were 410,000; the year's surplus is added to reserves.
