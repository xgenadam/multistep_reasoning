## Scope

Source: PLAN.md § Phase A. This is groundwork for R (persistence and resume),
P-P2 (snapshot correctness via `seq`/`ts`) and Q-02 (judging rehydrated
documents).

- Each op-log entry gets a monotonic `seq` (per `Document`) and a `ts` (UTC
  ISO). `to_dict`/`from_dict` round-trip them, and old dicts without them
  still load.
- Move the snapshot/rehydrate helpers out of `journal/baseline/harness.py`
  (≈ :257-366) into `reasoning_sub_agents/document/`. The harness then
  imports them, and its run-file format is unchanged.
- A-8 tests:
  - `seq` is strictly increasing across `apply_operations`, including
    after `clone()`;
  - snapshot → rehydrate is lossless on a multi-part fixture;
  - a tracked run file still rehydrates.
